What is StressTest?

StressTest is a West-Sussex based collaborative arts platform, acting as a production 'label' to develop, produce and stress test new artwork from multiple creatives, instead of relying on a static 'theatre company' model.

What does StressTest do?

With StressTest working as a platform for experimental and traditional work to be developed and stress tested, there is no cookie-cut plan for what types/styles of work StressTest might produce. Instead, it acts as an opportunity to take risks with our work via an established platform; whilst celebrating the impermanence and variety of art and artists in a constantly changing industry.
