Welcome To The Nest (Rehearsed Reading)
Written by Kane Heath
Directed by Lucy Sellers
2025 · Studio 5 - University Of Chichester

'Welcome To The Nest' was a rehearsed reading of a new stageplay inspired by Greek Mythology. It acted as a practical opportunity to showcase a proof of concept staging of new work and to be able to share and develop evolving material. Brought to life by a talented cast which we are very thankful for, and an incredible student director; It played to two sold out audiences at the University Of Chichester, originally only having one showing but opening a second performance after the intial date sold out. All performance photography by the talented @createv.bell on Instagram.
